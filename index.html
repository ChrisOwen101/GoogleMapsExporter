Your files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Get your saved places out of Google Maps.

Google lets you download your saved places, but the files are hard to use. Placefile puts every list and starred place in one easy page, then lets you download them as a spreadsheet or a map file.

FavouritesWant to goStarred places

if you don't have your file yet.

  1. Step 1

    Go to takeout.google.com and sign in to Google. Click Deselect all.

  2. Step 2

    Scroll down and tick Saved and Maps (your places). Click Next step, then Create export.

  3. Step 3

    Google emails you a download link, which can take a few minutes. Download the zip file and drop it here. Don't open it first.

  4. Good to know

    Places in your lists (like Want to go) arrive without a spot on the map. Starred and labelled places have one. Placefile can find the missing ones for you.
